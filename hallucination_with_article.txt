### **3. What are the four main categories of cars described in the article?**

Akerlof identifies four categories of cars:

- New good cars.
- New bad cars (lemons).
- Used good cars.
- Used bad cars (lemons).

The author distinguishes cars according to their age (new or used) and their quality (good or bad).



**Page reference:** p. 489, Section II.A – The Automobiles Market.&#x20;
