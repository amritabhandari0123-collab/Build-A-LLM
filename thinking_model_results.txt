# Step 6.3 – Thinking vs Non-Thinking Models

## Question

A retailer buys 1,247 units at €38.75 each. It sells 83% of them, rounded down to whole units, at €64.90 each with a 12% discount. Customers return 7% of the sold units, rounded down, for full refunds; those units cannot be resold. Unsold units sell for €21.40 each. Shipping costs €2.85 per unit originally bought, and fixed costs are €6,730. What is the final profit? Round only the final amount to two decimals.

## Test 1

Model: ChatGPT

Mode: Instant

Instruction: Give only the final answer.

Output:

€930.46

## Test 2

Model: ChatGPT

Mode: Instant

Instruction: Show the calculation before answering.

Output:

**Calculate the number of units**

- Units purchased: 1,247
- Units sold: 1,247 × 83% = 1,035.01 → 1,035 units
- Units returned: 1,035 × 7% = 72.45 → 72 units
- Net units sold: 1,035 − 72 = 963 units
- Unsold units: 1,247 − 1,035 = 212 units

**2. Calculate total revenue**

Discounted selling price:

€64.90 × (1 − 0.12) = €57.112

Revenue after refunds:

963 × €57.112 = €54,998.856

Revenue from unsold units:

212 × €21.40 = €4,536.80

Total revenue = €59,535.656

**3. Calculate total costs**

- Purchase cost: 1,247 × €38.75 = €48,321.25
- Shipping cost: 1,247 × €2.85 = €3,553.95
- Fixed costs: €6,730

Total costs = €58,605.20

**4. Calculate final profit**

Profit = Total revenue − Total costs

= €59,535.656 − €58,605.20

= €930.456

Final profit = €930.46

## Test 3

Model: ChatGPT

Mode: Thinking

Instruction: Give only the final answer.

Output:

€930.46

## Test 4

Model: ChatGPT

Mode: Thinking

Instruction: Show the calculation before answering.

Output:

**Calculate units sold and returned**

- Units sold: 1,247 × 0.83 = 1,035.01 → 1,035 units
- Units returned: 1,035 × 0.07 = 72.45 → 72 units
- Net units sold: 1,035 − 72 = 963 units
- Unsold units: 1,247 − 1,035 = 212 units

**2. Calculate revenue**

Discounted selling price = €64.90 × 0.88 = €57.112

Revenue after refunds = 963 × €57.112 = €54,998.856

Revenue from unsold units = 212 × €21.40 = €4,536.80

Total revenue = €59,535.656

**3. Calculate costs**

Purchase cost = 1,247 × €38.75 = €48,321.25

Shipping cost = 1,247 × €2.85 = €3,553.95

Fixed costs = €6,730

Total costs = €58,605.20

**4. Calculate final profit**

Profit = Total revenue − Total costs

= €59,535.656 − €58,605.20

= €930.456

Final profit = €930.46

## Verification

The correct calculation is:

963 × 57.112 + 212 × 21.40 − 1,247 × 38.75 − 1,247 × 2.85 − 6,730 = €930.456

Correct final profit: €930.46

## Comparison and conclusion

All four tests produced the correct final profit of €930.46. I did not observe any difference in accuracy between the Instant and Thinking modes for this particular question. When I asked for only the final answer, both modes provided a single numerical result. When I asked them to show the calculations, both provided detailed explanations of the units sold, returns, revenue and costs. The detailed responses were more useful because I could check the individual calculations rather than simply trust the final answer. However, since this experiment involved only one question, I cannot conclude that both modes are equally accurate for all types of calculations.

Model identification: ChatGPT Instant and Thinking modes were used. The exact underlying model versions were not independently recorded.
